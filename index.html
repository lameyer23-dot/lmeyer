Greetings from Gemütlich Inn!

Gemütlich Inn is a small inn located in the small town Walenstadt in eastern Switzerland.

More about our Inn:

Our inn is a locally owned bed and breakfast that offers a relaxing get away near the mountains.

Gemütlich Inn has been around for over 50 years, offering a place to stay for those passing through, or even natives wanting to get away from the house.

With tempatures ranging from warm to chilly, Walenstadt is the perfect place to reset and unwind.
