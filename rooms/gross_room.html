Groß Room

Our Groß Room has more room for more than one person, with two twin-sized beds that give plenty of space for multiple people to spend the evening together. Our inn also offers a microwave, a minifridge, personal closet and storage, and more. You will also be able to find complimentary tea and coffee in the lobby, as well as complimentary breakfast in the mornings.
